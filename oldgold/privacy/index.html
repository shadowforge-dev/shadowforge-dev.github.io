OldGold

Privacy Policy
Effective: March 24, 2026

Overview

OldGold is a Safari extension for old.reddit.com developed by ShadowForge LLC. Your privacy matters. This policy explains what data OldGold accesses, stores, and shares — which is very little.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect personal information
  • We do not use analytics or tracking
  • We do not serve ads
  • We do not sell, share, or transmit any user data to third parties
  • We do not have servers that receive your data (OldGold is fully client-side)

Data Stored on Your Device

OldGold stores your preferences (theme selection, tweak toggles, filter lists, layout settings) locally in your browser's extension storage. This data never leaves your device except through iCloud sync as described below.

iCloud Sync

OldGold uses Apple's iCloud Key-Value Storage to sync your settings across your devices. This data is transmitted directly between your devices through Apple's iCloud infrastructure. ShadowForge LLC does not have access to your iCloud data. You can disable iCloud sync in your device's system settings.

Synced data includes:

  • Theme and tweak preferences
  • Subreddit and text filter lists
  • Layout and thumbnail settings
  • NSFW filter state

Web Content

OldGold runs only on old.reddit.com and related Reddit domains. The extension modifies the visual appearance of pages you visit but does not intercept, log, or transmit any browsing data, login credentials, or page content.

Children's Privacy

OldGold does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The extension enhances Reddit, which has its own age requirements.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the app. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
