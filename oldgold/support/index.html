FAQ

How do I enable the extension?

On iOS: Settings → Safari → Extensions → OldGold → toggle on. On macOS: Safari → Settings → Extensions → check OldGold.

Why don't my settings sync?

Make sure iCloud is enabled on all your devices: Settings → [your name] → iCloud. OldGold syncs via iCloud Key-Value Storage. Changes may take a moment to propagate.

The extension doesn't work on new Reddit

OldGold is designed exclusively for old.reddit.com. Visit old.reddit.com or set your Reddit preferences to use the old design.

How do I change themes?

Open the OldGold companion app and go to Settings, or use the Safari toolbar popup for quick switching.
