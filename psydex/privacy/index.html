Psydex

Privacy Policy
Effective: March 24, 2026

Overview

Psydex is a Pokemon Trading Card Game companion app for iOS developed by ShadowForge LLC. This policy explains what data Psydex accesses, stores, and shares.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect personal information or require an account
  • We do not use third-party analytics or tracking services
  • We do not serve ads
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties

Data Stored on Your Device

Psydex stores the following locally on your device:

  • Your card collections, wishlists, and trade binders
  • Card ownership details (condition, finish, purchase price, notes)
  • Search history and recently viewed cards
  • App preferences and theme settings

iCloud Sync

Psydex uses Apple's CloudKit to sync your collection data across your devices. This data is transmitted directly through Apple's iCloud infrastructure. ShadowForge LLC does not have access to your iCloud data. You can manage iCloud sync in your device's system settings.

Psydex API

Psydex connects to ShadowForge's API servers to retrieve Pokemon TCG card data, including card details, set information, pricing data, and Pokemon species information. These requests include:

  • Search queries (card names, set names, filter criteria)
  • Your device's preferred language (to serve localized card data)
  • Standard HTTP request metadata (IP address, user agent)

We do not associate API requests with individual users. No collection data, personal information, or account identifiers are transmitted to our servers. Server logs are retained for operational purposes only and are not used for tracking.

Pricing Data

Card pricing information displayed in Psydex is sourced from publicly available market data (TCGPlayer, Cardmarket). Price data is retrieved through ShadowForge's API — your device does not connect to third-party pricing services directly.

Images and Assets

Card images and Pokemon sprites are served through our API and cached locally on your device for offline access and performance. Cached images can be cleared through your device's storage settings.

Children's Privacy

Psydex does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app does not require an account or collect personal data from any user.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the app or our services. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
