FAQ

What data does Psydex include?

Psydex includes Pokemon TCG card data from multiple sources, covering sets, card details, pricing, and Pokemon species information. Card data is available in 11 languages.

How does collection sync work?

Collections sync automatically via iCloud. Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID on all your devices and that iCloud is enabled for Psydex in Settings.

Are card prices accurate?

Pricing data is sourced from TCGPlayer and Cardmarket and is updated regularly. Prices reflect recent market trends but may not match real-time listings. Use pricing as a reference, not a guarantee.

Can I use Psydex offline?

Previously viewed cards and your collections are available offline. Searching for new cards or viewing updated prices requires an internet connection.

A card is missing or has wrong data

Our database is continuously updated. If you notice missing or incorrect card data, please email us at help@shadowforge.dev with the card name and set, and we'll investigate.
