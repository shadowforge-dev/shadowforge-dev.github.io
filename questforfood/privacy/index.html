Quest for Food

Privacy Policy
Effective: August 24, 2026

Overview

Quest for Food is a website that helps you decide where to eat by rolling a die over nearby restaurants, developed by ShadowForge LLC. There is no account, no sign-in, and nothing to install. To find restaurants near you it needs to know roughly where you are, and that is the only personal information involved. This policy explains exactly what happens to it.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require or offer an account
  • We do not use analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising identifiers
  • We do not serve ads
  • We do not set cookies
  • We do not sell or share your information with anyone
  • We do not store your location, your searches, or the restaurants you were shown
  • We do not build a profile of you or keep a history of your visits

Location

When you tap the roll button, your browser asks whether you want to share your location. That prompt comes from your browser, not from us, and you can decline it.

If you allow it, your coordinates are sent to our server and passed straight to the Google Places API to find restaurants near you. We use the result to work out how far away each restaurant is, and then we discard it. Your coordinates are not written to a database, not kept in a log, and not associated with you.

If you decline, we fall back to an approximate location derived from your IP address by Cloudflare, our hosting provider. This is typically accurate to the city and is not stored either.

If you enter a ZIP code, it is sent to the Google Geocoding API to turn it into coordinates, which are then used exactly as above. A ZIP code you type is remembered in your own browser's local storage so you do not have to type it again. That value stays on your device, is never sent anywhere except as part of a search you asked for, and is cleared when you clear your browser's site data.

Restaurant Information

Restaurant names, addresses, categories and map links come from Google. We request them for a single search and hold them only in your browser's memory for as long as that page is open. Closing or reloading the page discards them. We do not save them, and we do not build our own copy of Google's data.

Google's handling of the requests we make on your behalf is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Preferences Stored on Your Device

Two small values may be kept in your browser's local storage: a ZIP code if you entered one, and your chosen theme. Both stay on your device. Neither is transmitted to us or to anyone else, and clearing your browser's site data removes them.

Feedback You Send Us

If you use the form on the support page, we receive the category and message you wrote, plus your email address only if you chose to provide one. We use it to read and respond to your feedback, and nothing else. Leaving the email field blank means we have no way to identify you, which is a perfectly good option if you do not need a reply.

Hosting and Logs

The site is served by Cloudflare. As with any web host, Cloudflare processes connection information such as IP addresses in order to deliver the page and protect the site from abuse. This is standard infrastructure logging that we do not control, do not use for analytics, and do not combine with anything else. Cloudflare's practices are described in the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Children

Quest for Food is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Since we do not collect personal information from anyone, there is nothing held about a child to request the deletion of.

Your Choices

  • Decline the location prompt — the site still works using an approximate location
  • Enter a ZIP code instead, if you would rather not share precise location at all
  • Clear your browser's site data to remove the ZIP code and theme stored on your device
  • Revoke location permission at any time in your browser's site settings

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Since we hold nothing about you, a change here cannot alter what has already happened to data we never kept.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the support page.
