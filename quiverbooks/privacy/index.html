QuiverBooks

Privacy Policy
Effective: March 24, 2026

Overview

QuiverBooks is a multi-format e-reader for iOS and macOS developed by ShadowForge LLC. We believe your reading life is your business. This policy explains what data QuiverBooks accesses, stores, and shares.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect personal information
  • We do not use analytics or tracking
  • We do not serve ads
  • We do not sell, share, or transmit user data to third parties
  • We do not have access to the books you import or read

Data Stored on Your Device

All data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework:

  • Your book library (imported files, metadata, cover images)
  • Reading progress, bookmarks, and highlights
  • Collections and organizational data
  • App preferences and theme settings

Your books and reading data remain on your device and under your control.

iCloud Sync

QuiverBooks uses Apple's iCloud Key-Value Storage to sync reading statistics (pages read, reading time, streaks) across your devices. This data is transmitted directly through Apple's iCloud infrastructure. ShadowForge LLC does not have access to your iCloud data.

Open Library API

QuiverBooks may query the Open Library API (openlibrary.org) to enrich book metadata (title, author, cover art) when you import a book. These requests include only the book's ISBN or title — no personal information is sent. Open Library is a project of the Internet Archive and is governed by their own privacy policy.

File Access

QuiverBooks requests read-only access to files you explicitly select for import (EPUB, MOBI, PDF, CBZ). The app does not scan, index, or access files beyond what you choose to open.

Children's Privacy

QuiverBooks does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app does not require an account or collect personal data from any user.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the app. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
