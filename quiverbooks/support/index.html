Supported Formats

What file formats can I read?

QuiverBooks supports EPUB, MOBI, AZW (Kindle), PDF, and CBZ (comic books). All DRM-free content.

FAQ

How do I import books?

Tap the import button in the Library tab. You can import from Files, iCloud Drive, Dropbox, or any connected cloud storage. You can also use the iOS Share Sheet from other apps or drag-and-drop on macOS.

Can I read DRM-protected books?

No. QuiverBooks supports DRM-free files only. Books purchased from stores with DRM (like most Kindle purchases) cannot be opened. Consider purchasing from DRM-free retailers.

Where are my books stored?

Books are stored locally on your device in the app's sandboxed storage. They are included in your device backups.

My reading progress seems wrong

Reading progress is calculated based on your position in the book. For EPUB/MOBI files, this is based on chapter and scroll position. For PDFs, it's based on page number. If progress seems off after an import, try opening the book and navigating to where you left off.
