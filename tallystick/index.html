Tallystick

The scorepad that knows what you're playing

Most scorekeeping apps are a grid of empty boxes. Tallystick knows that Cribbage stops at 121, that Hearts ends on a high score but is won on a low one, and that in Phases you win by finishing the ladder rather than by the points. Pick a game and it already knows how it's scored.

Nineteen games

Cribbage · Hearts · Spades · Euchre · Rummy · Gin · Canasta · Hand & Foot · Golf · Pinochle · Pitch · Mexican Train · Crowns · Phases · Rolling Sixes · Trickster · Low Twelve · Crows · Pounce

Search by the name on the box and you'll find it, whatever we had to call it. If yours isn't there, make it — the empty search result offers to.

Fix a score from three rounds ago

Every number you've entered stays on screen and stays tappable. Tap it, change it, and every total re-adds itself, including who's winning. Finishing a game isn't a one-way door either.

Built for a table, not a spreadsheet

Big keys, one-tap values for the scores that come up constantly, and a plus key so you can enter 10+10+5 without doing it in your head. The screen stays awake while you play, so the phone can sit on the table between rounds. Nobody fills in a form before they can score — names are editable at the table, and changing one carries every score with it.

What's free

Every game in the catalogue. Any number of players. Correcting any round. Portrait and landscape. However many games you play. These aren't a trial.

What Pro adds

Pro keeps the history: several games on the go at once, finished games kept with win rates and head-to-head records, evenings gathered together, versions of games played your way, and colour throughout. One payment. It never expires, it isn't a subscription, and there are no adverts anywhere in the app.

Your record is yours

Everything is stored on your device. No account, no sign-in, nothing sent anywhere — Tallystick makes no network requests at all. Export the whole record to a file whenever you like, and restore it on another phone.
