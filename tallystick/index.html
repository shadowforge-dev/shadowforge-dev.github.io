Most scorekeeping apps are a grid of empty boxes. Tallystick knows that Cribbage stops at 121, that Hearts ends on a high score but is won on a low one, and that in Phases you win by finishing the ladder rather than by the points. Pick a game and it already knows how it's scored.

Search by the name on the box and you'll find it, whatever we had to call it. If yours isn't there, make it: the empty search result offers to.

Features

Built for a card table, not a spreadsheet

Fix Any Round

Every number you've entered stays on screen and stays tappable. Change a score from three rounds ago and every total re-adds itself, including who's winning.

A Keypad for the Table

Big keys, one-tap values for the scores that come up constantly, and a plus key for 10+10+5. The screen stays awake between rounds.

No Forms First

Tap a game and start scoring. Rename players at the table, swap someone in mid-game, and follow whose deal it is.

When It Ends

Final standings and a few honest lines about how it went: the margin, a standout round, a comeback. Share a picture of it with the table, free.

The RecordPRO

Finished games kept, with win rates, head-to-head records, and evenings gathered together. Several games on the go at once.

Yours, on Your Phone

No account, no sign-in, and no network requests at all. Export the whole record to a file and restore it on another phone.
