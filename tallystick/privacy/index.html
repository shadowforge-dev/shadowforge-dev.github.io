Tallystick

Privacy Policy
Effective: August 16, 2026

Overview

Tallystick is a scorekeeping app for card and dice games on iPhone, developed by ShadowForge LLC. It has no account, no sign-in, and no server. Everything you enter stays on your device. This policy explains what that means in detail, which does not take long.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect personal information
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, or advertising identifiers
  • We do not serve ads
  • We do not sell, share, or transmit any user data to third parties
  • We have no servers that receive your data — Tallystick does not make network requests
  • We do not require or offer an account
  • Tallystick contains no third-party SDKs or analytics libraries of any kind

Data Stored on Your Device

Tallystick keeps its record in your app's own storage, on your device. That includes the games you are playing, games you have finished, the names and colours of the people you play with, games you have invented or renamed, and your preferences. None of it leaves your device unless you export it yourself.

The names you type for players are exactly that — text you chose. Tallystick does not ask for, look up, or import contacts.

iCloud Sync

One setting syncs across your devices through Apple's iCloud Key-Value Storage: whether the app is set to Light, Dark, or Auto. That is all. Your games, your record, the people you play with, and your purchases are not synced by the app and remain on the device that created them.

This data is transmitted directly between your devices through Apple's iCloud infrastructure. ShadowForge LLC has no access to it. You can disable iCloud sync in your device's system settings.

Backups You Make Yourself

Tallystick can export your whole record to a file, and restore from one. That file is created only when you ask for it, and it goes wherever you send it — AirDrop, Files, or anywhere else you choose. ShadowForge LLC never receives it. An ordinary encrypted device backup through iCloud or a computer will also include Tallystick's data, in the same way it includes any app's.

In-App Purchases

Tallystick offers a one-time Pro unlock and an optional tip jar, processed entirely through Apple's App Store. ShadowForge LLC does not collect, receive, or store payment information. Whether you have purchased Pro is determined by asking Apple's StoreKit on your device; we keep no record of it ourselves and no purchase list of our own.

Diagnostics

If you choose to report a problem through Apple's App Store, or share crash and usage diagnostics with developers in your device's privacy settings, Apple may provide us with aggregate diagnostic reports. These come from Apple, are not linked to you by us, and are governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

Children's Privacy

Tallystick does not knowingly collect information from children under 13, and it collects no personal information from anybody. It is a scorepad; children are welcome to play.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the app. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
